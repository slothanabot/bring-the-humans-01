A bright, warm, friendly ad for [make.fun](https://make.fun): a single
self-contained page (`index.html`) inviting friends to join, build together,
and earn together. No build step, no framework, no network fetches — just
HTML and CSS in one file.

1. Share the link — `https://make.fun/r/x15bjha9` — with friends who code.
2. They sign up, pick a bounty, and ship real code.
3. Verified signups through the link earn referral rewards, and your friends
   earn bounties of their own. The top 100 referrers in this round share a
   $1,000 prize pool.
